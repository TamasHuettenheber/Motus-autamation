# Series Bible – Jori & Flimm

## Non-negotiable boundary
The published book "Die 24 verschwundenen Weihnachtslichter" contains 24 connected missions and 48 tasks. Those plots, tasks, answers, light-letter sequence and final message are reserved for the paid book/e-book.

Video episodes must be newly written and must not reproduce or summarize a book mission.

## Positioning
The short-video series is a prequel/side-story universe that explains how Jori and Flimm became a team and explores Flockenfels before and around their larger adventures.

## Audience
Children approximately 7–10 years old, plus parents who discover the content with them.

## Tone
- warm
- adventurous
- curious
- safe
- lightly magical
- never frightening
- intelligent without sounding school-like
- humor may come from Flimm's reactions

## Character anchors

### Jori
Role: curious young problem-solver and emotional anchor.

Visual anchor from the current cover:
- child with dark curly hair
- red knitted winter hat
- dark blue winter coat
- red/cream striped scarf
- practical winter boots
- small shoulder satchel
- expressive warm face
- adventurous but kind, never reckless

Behavior:
- observes before deciding
- explains clues in child-friendly language
- listens to others
- admits when a first idea is wrong
- solves through logic, empathy and experimentation

### Flimm
Role: small magical companion and comic/light-hearted counterpoint.

Visual anchor from the current cover:
- small round lantern-like creature
- warm brown/golden body
- glowing star-shaped light in the belly
- red/green knitted winter cap
- short arms and legs
- very expressive eyes

Behavior:
- curious, impulsive, affectionate
- light reacts subtly to emotion
- may misunderstand ordinary objects in funny ways
- should help, not solve every riddle

### Murrflaum
Book-canonical third figure. Use sparingly in free videos until the series naturally reaches him.

## Flockenfels
A snowy, cozy winter town with:
- warm lantern light
- steep roofs
- narrow lanes
- towers
- small workshops and shops
- forests and snowy hills nearby
- magical details that feel discoverable rather than spectacular

Avoid making every episode Christmas-specific. The world should survive beyond December.

## Episode grammar
Every episode should contain:
1. Hook in first 2–4 seconds.
2. A concrete mini-problem.
3. Jori and Flimm investigate.
4. One visual or verbal riddle.
5. 5–10 second thinking pause; encode the wait as an explicit silence and hold the riddle caption until the answer.
6. Answer or partial answer.
7. Story payoff.
8. Small cliffhanger or forward link.
9. Soft CTA only when useful.

## Riddle rules
- exactly one core riddle per short
- solvable from information shown/heard in the video
- no trick questions that rely on missing information
- age-appropriate for 7–10
- rotate types: pattern, counting, logic, direction, word, visual difference, simple code
- keep an episode ledger to avoid near-duplicates

## Continuity rules
- every episode receives an episode number and continuity note
- maintain a "known facts" list
- do not reset character relationships
- do not contradict previous episodes
- do not reveal book-only solutions or plot twists

## Initial story arc
Arc 1: "Bevor das große Lichtabenteuer begann"
Purpose: establish how Jori and Flimm became partners.

Suggested beats:
- Jori notices a strange warm glow where no lantern should be.
- Flimm is hiding/looking for something small and harmless.
- They initially misunderstand each other.
- A tiny puzzle forces cooperation.
- They discover that Flockenfels contains old paths, signs and light-magic.
- Their partnership grows over several episodes.

This arc must remain distinct from the book's December mission sequence.
